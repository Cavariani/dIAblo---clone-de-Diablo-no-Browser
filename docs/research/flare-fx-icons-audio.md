Conventions match `characters.json`: direction rows are `0=W, 1=NW, 2=N, 3=NE, 4=E, 5=SE, 6=S, 7=SW`. A frame is drawn at `(anchor - ox, anchor - oy)`. `1x1` frames are intentional blanks. `scripts/assets/parse-flare-anim.mjs` parses these `.txt` files.

## 3. Ground loot (`animations/loot`, 39 files)

- Every loot animation is 6 frames, 600 ms, `play_once`, direction 0 only.
- Frames f0-f4 are the toss arc (up to about 250 px above the anchor). Frame f5 is the resting pose; hold it.
- Gold piles by amount: `coins5` for 1-24, `coins25` for 25-99, `coins100` for 100 and up.
- Armor shares 4 sprites (`clothes`, `leather_armor`, `steel_armor`, `boots`) plus `belt`.
- Rings use `ring`. Amulets and gems use `gem` (white; tint it).
- Missing: crossbow, orb, quiver, and a dedicated helm sprite.
- The engine drop sound is `soundfx/flying_loot.ogg`. Each item plays its own `inventory_*.ogg` when handled.

## 6. FX to generate procedurally (particles / shaders)

1. Beams and chains: jagged lightning arcs between targets, the blood-siphon beam, and an arcane channel beam. Use a mesh strip + noise shader.
2. Shockwave rings: war cry and nova edges. Use an expanding ellipse with a distortion/refraction ring.
3. Whirlwind spin trail and charge motion trails (ribbon trails). Evasive roll afterimages (ghost sprites of the character).
4. Dust puffs on landing, run and roll. Blood splatter and gore on crit death. Bone shard debris and orbiting bone shards.
5. Meteor fall streak and scorch decal. Ground burn / frost / poison decals that persist on the floor.
6. Blizzard falling snow volume. Poison cloud tinting. Curse swirl over enemies.
7. Legendary drop beam (colored vertical light pillar + sparkle) and item-quality ground labels and glow.
8. Resource orbs fill (HP/MP globes: Flare has only horizontal bars, no orbs). Level-up burst. Waypoint / portal swirl (Flare has none).
9. Hit flash (white tint), elemental tint overlays, damage numbers, screen shake. Additive glow for all fire, lightning and arcane sprites.
10. Sentry turret and bone golem bodies. No suitable Flare sprite exists for either.
